A walk around downtown

Bushnell, about 1880

Two railroads crossed in the middle of town. East Main is the wide street on the east side of the Burlington tracks, and West Main is the wide street on the west side. Hail Street runs north of Hurst Street, and that is where the stores and the depot stood. On the town view, the compass points to true north.
