# Block audit

Checklist for the Sanborn comparison. The model has 30 platted rectangles in `public/data/bushnell-1880.json`. The fire-insurance maps number their own blocks. A row is finished only when the lots were compared. “No data found” means the sheet was opened and the footprint or the name could not be read. Those lots are left as they were.

Sheets used: January 1886 (3 sheets), August 1893 (4), May 1898 (4), March 1906 (5), February 1912 (8). All 24 overviews were opened. Crops were read for the blocks marked compared.

## Finished lot comparison (8 of 30 plat blocks)

### 72 — Barnes to Hurst, East Main to Crawford

Plat id `barnes-hurst__crafford-east-main`. The model had called this rectangle the East Park. The sheets draw shops on East Main and houses on Crawford.

Sheets: 1886 sheet 2, 1893 sheet 2, 1898 sheet 2, 1906 sheet 3, 1912 sheet 4.

- 1886: frame shops on East Main, already in the 1880 file. Five frame dwellings on the Crawford side, added to the 1880 file as `block72-crawford-1886-1` through `5`. No family names.
- 1893: the East Main row is the brick block already in the 1890 patch. The five Crawford dwellings are still there. Nothing removed on this side.
- 1898: a dwelling is added on lot 2, Crawford side (`block72-lot2-1898`). The 1893 sheet shows the cigar factory there and no house.
- 1906: that house is replaced by the two-story lodgings (`block72-lodgings-1906`).
- 1912: dwellings still on this side. No further lot was read as empty.

Unreadable: the story count on every rear shed. No data found.

### 80 — Davis to Barnes, East Main to Crawford

Plat id `davis-barnes__crafford-east-main`.

Sheets: 1886 sheet 2, 1893 sheet 2, 1898 sheet 2, 1906 sheet 3, 1912 sheet 4.

- 1886: Baptist church in the middle of the Crawford side. One-story dwelling at the Davis end of East Main, lot 316 (`b80-316-1886`). Other Crawford dwellings were already stand-ins and were left.
- 1893: lot 316 is still one story. The church is still there.
- 1898: lot 316 is replaced by a two-story dwelling (`b80-316-1898`). New England Bottle Works is drawn beside it. That works is not given a separate building. No data found for its footprint.
- 1906 and 1912: the two-story dwelling and the church are still there.

Unreadable: which of the small Crawford footprints changed story between 1886 and 1893. No data found. They were not swapped.

### 59 — Hurst to Hail, East Main to Crawford

Plat id `hurst-hail__crafford-east-main`. High-resolution crops of sheet 3 (1886, 1893, 1898) and 1906 sheet 4.

Crawford side, Hurst toward Hail:

- 1886: National Hotel, brick, at the Hurst end. Three frame dwellings further along Crawford. A two-story wagon shop and the marble works are not dwellings. The hotel is moved to this corner. The three dwellings are `block59-crawford-1886-1` through `3`.
- 1893: the hotel is marked three stories. The same three dwellings remain. The wagon shop remains.
- 1898: the hotel and the three dwellings remain. W. J. Vertree’s fence factory is labeled on the Hurst side. No data found for its footprint, so it is not drawn.
- 1906: the hotel and the dwellings remain. Jas. Cole Flats, two stories, brick, is labeled on lot 306 toward Hail (`jas-cole-flats-1906`).
- 1912: this block was not on the crop. No data found for a 1912 change.

Unreadable: the depth of each dwelling behind the street. No data found. The East Main store names were not copied door by door in this pass.

### 48 — Hail to Gridley, East Main to Crawford

Plat id `hail-gridley__crafford-east-main`. Same high-resolution crops.

Crawford side, lots 307 to 312 (Hail toward Gridley):

- 1886: frame dwellings on 307, 308, 309, 310, and 311. Lot 312, the Gridley end, shows sheds and no dwelling. The stand-in house on that lot was removed.
- 1893: a two-story frame dwelling is on lot 312 (`block48-lot312-1893`). The other five dwellings are still there.
- 1898: the six dwellings are still there. The Dutch Reformed church is labeled at Gridley, already in the model.
- 1906: six dwellings still on Crawford. Several are marked two stories. No data found for which of the five older houses changed story, so those stand-ins were not swapped.
- 1912: not cropped. No data found.

### 60 — Hurst to Hail, West Main to Dean

Plat id `hurst-hail__west-main-dean`. High-resolution crops of 1886 sheet 3 and 1906 sheet 4.

Dean side:

- 1886: Campbellite Meeting House, brick, on lot 301 at Hurst. The Christian Church already in the file is moved to that corner, and the sheet’s label is used on the sign. Two frame dwellings, about lots 303 and 305 (`block60-dean-1886-1` and `2`).
- 1906: the meeting house is still labeled. A two-story dwelling is on lot 302, which the 1886 sheet does not show as a house. F. M. Black’s livery is labeled behind the meeting house (`fm-black-livery-1906`). The 1886 and 1893 sheets for the years in between were not both read for that livery. It is drawn from 1906.
- 1893 and 1898: not cropped for this block in this pass. No data found for a change between those two years.
- West Main is the brick store row on both sheets. Door-by-door names were not copied. No data found for a store that was replaced.

### 47 — Hail to Gridley, West Main to Dean

Plat id `hail-gridley__west-main-dean`. High-resolution crop of 1906 sheet 4, and the 1886 sheet 3 crop of the same block.

- 1886 and 1906 both show frame dwellings along Dean. The 1906 sheet has a dwelling on each of lots 306, 307, 308, 309, and 310. The model already has houses along that side. No lot was read as empty on one sheet and filled on the other, so none of those houses was added or removed.
- Unreadable: which Dean house changed from one story to two. No data found.

### 81 — Davis to Barnes, West Main to Dean

Plat id `davis-barnes__west-main-dean`. High-resolution crops of sheet 2 for 1886, 1893, and 1898, and 1906 sheet 3.

Dean side, lots 201 to 207:

- 1886: Excelsior Mills on lots 201 to 203. Three frame dwellings further toward Barnes (`block81-dean-1886-1` through `3`).
- 1893: the mill is still labeled, and the note says it is not running. The three dwellings remain.
- 1898: the mill is gone. Two frame dwellings stand on lot 201 (`block81-dean-1898-1` and `2`). A steam laundry is labeled on the alley side of those lots (`home-steam-laundry-1898`). The 1906 sheet calls it Home Steam Laundry.
- J. W. Roach’s livery stays on the West Main side. It was already in the file.

### 71 — Barnes to Hurst, West Main to Dean

Plat id `barnes-hurst__west-main-dean`. Same high-resolution crops as block 81.

Dean side, lots 208 to 213:

- 1886: frame dwellings on lots 209 and 211 (`block71-dean-1886-1` and `2`). Presbyterian Church on the Hurst end. The church is moved onto this block. Lots 208, 210, and 212 have no dwelling.
- 1893: lots 208 and 209 are empty, so the lot 209 house is removed. A dwelling is on lot 210 (`block71-dean-1893-1`). The lot 211 house remains. A one-and-a-half-story dwelling is on the Dean front of lot 213, north of the church (`block71-dean-1893-2`).
- 1898 and 1906: the church and the Dean dwellings remain. The 1906 sheet labels the church Presby. Church.
- West Main holds Hotel Alexander, already in the file. The 1886 sheet labels that building Hendee House.
